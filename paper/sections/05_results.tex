\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Main comparison}

Table~\ref{tab:main-results} summarizes the corrected phase-interleaved
results.  EfficientNet-B0 improves the primary metric over the published
\cbramod{} reference on five datasets (CHB-MIT, TUEV, ISRUC, BCIC2020-3, and
MentalArithmetic), is effectively tied on PhysioNet-MI and SEED-V, and is
lower on TUAB, SHU-MI, Mumtaz2016, and FACED.  These comparisons use published
CBraMod references rather than same-pipeline reruns, so they should be read as
evidence of competitiveness rather than a definitive paired ranking.

\paragraph{Reliable gains.}
TUEV is the clearest confirmation that the result is not one favorable seed:
all three $\kappa$ values (.6871, .7566, and .7053) exceed the .6744 reference,
and the mean F1 also improves.  ISRUC, CHB-MIT, and BCIC2020-3 provide further
primary-metric gains of 1.95, 7.37, and 9.23 percentage points, respectively.
MentalArithmetic gains 2.45 PR-AUC points and 4.61 AUROC points, although its
standard deviation remains the largest in the table.

\paragraph{Near parity.}
PhysioNet-MI is effectively tied in $\kappa$ and improves F1 from .6427 to
.6448.  This result uses $P=1$, showing that folding is not forced when the
unfolded 64-row geometry is already compatible with the CNN.  SEED-V is also at
parity (mean $\kappa$ .2565 versus .2569, with a slightly higher F1), which is
why we report the three-seed mean rather than a best-seed value.

\paragraph{Where generic geometry is insufficient.}
FACED remains the largest gap, with a 13.71-point $\kappa$ deficit.  The result
cannot be explained by model size alone because the same encoder is competitive
on another emotion dataset, SEED-V.  FACED may depend more strongly on subject
shift, amplitude distribution, physical electrode topology, or spatially
distributed affective patterns.  Mumtaz2016 is less diagnostic because both
methods operate near the ceiling, but \cbramod{} remains consistently higher.

\subsection{Result stability}

The standard deviations reveal two regimes.  ISRUC, Mumtaz2016, TUAB, and
SEED-V are comparatively stable under seed changes, while TUEV, FACED, SHU-MI,
and especially MentalArithmetic are more sensitive.  MentalArithmetic has only
1,343 training examples and 172 validation examples; its validation-selected
checkpoint can therefore change substantially with minibatch order and
initialization.  The reported .6512 PR-AUC is the three-seed mean, not an
exploratory best-seed or best-epoch value.

\subsection{Parameter efficiency}

After removing the unused ImageNet classifier, EfficientNet-B0 contains
approximately 4.01M encoder parameters, plus a small task head.  This is close
to the reported 4.9M parameters of \cbramod{}, but our model uses no EEG
self-supervised pretraining.  \reve{}-Base is substantially larger and is
pretrained on a much broader EEG corpus.  The comparison therefore isolates a
useful point: at compact scale and under full fine-tuning, input geometry and
generic convolutional features can account for a large fraction of downstream
performance.  It does not address frozen transfer, where large EEG foundation
models are expected to retain an advantage.
