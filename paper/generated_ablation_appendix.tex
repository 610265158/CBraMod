visual initialization, but not a uniform replacement for EEG pretraining.

\paragraph{Discussion.}
The phase-folding control shows a consistent advantage for CNN backbones across
most datasets, with the largest differences on FACED and MentalArithmetic and
smaller but positive differences on clinical and sleep tasks. ViT-Small is more
task-sensitive: it benefits clearly on TUEV, HMC, and SHU-MI, but contiguous
reshape is better on CHB-MIT and nearly tied on FACED and MentalArithmetic.
Thus the gain is not explained by height expansion alone; it depends on how the
backbone uses the phase-structured neighborhoods.
