## figure-01-main-comparison — Δ primary metric per dataset

- **Purpose**: show the signed effect of the new recipe (true cls-token + lr 5e-4 +
  clip 1) versus the old flatten baseline for every dataset in one view.
- **Plotted variables**: x = Δ primary metric in percentage points (pp), sorted;
  y = dataset (primary metric in parentheses). Bars colored by sign; significance
  stars from the Holm-corrected Welch test; exact Δ annotated.
- **Error bars**: none (Δ of means; CIs are in figure-02).
- **Caption draft**: "Change in the primary metric (pr_auc for binary, Cohen's κ for
  multiclass tasks) of the corrected CLS-token head with lr 5e-4 / clip 1 relative to
  the previously recorded flatten head, per dataset. Stars mark Holm-corrected
  significance (n=5+5)."
- **Key observation**: three datasets show large, corrected-significant gains
  (PhysioNet-MI +8.06, FACED +6.47, TUAB +2.48) plus ISRUC +0.90*; the largest raw
  gain is MentalArithmetic +15.10 (ns after correction); two datasets are negative
  (SEED-V −1.66, BCIC2020-3 −5.63, both ns); HMC/Mumtaz are at parity.
- **Interpretation checklist**:
  1. Why: one-glance summary of where the head/lr fix helps and where it does not.
  2. Notice: the effect is dataset-dependent — not a uniform upgrade.
  3. Decision change: per-dataset head selection should use cls_token for
     PhysioNet/FACED/TUAB/ISRUC/MentalArithmetic; keep flatten for BCIC2020-3;
     revisit SEED-V.
- **Caveats**: uncorrected p for annotated ns bars; Δ from unpaired summary-stat tests;
  baseline for TUAB is the superseded flatten head (see report §4).

## figure-02-forest-plot — effect sizes with 95% CIs

- **Purpose**: quantify uncertainty of each Δ; separate real effects from noise.
- **Plotted variables**: x = Δ primary metric (pp) with 95% CI (Welch, t-based);
  y = dataset sorted as figure-01. Filled markers = Holm-corrected p<.05; open = not
  significant.
- **Caption draft**: "Forest plot of the primary-metric difference (new − old flatten)
  with 95% confidence intervals; filled markers survive Holm correction across the 12
  datasets."
- **Key observation**: PhysioNet-MI/FACED/TUAB/ISRUC CIs exclude zero by wide margins;
  MentalArithmetic's CI (±10.1pp) crosses zero despite the largest point estimate;
  SEED-V's negative CI (±1.29pp) spans zero at the corrected level.
- **Interpretation checklist**:
  1. Why: separates "big number" from "reliable number".
  2. Notice: MentalArithmetic is the clearest case of large-but-unstable; SEED-V is
     consistently negative but small.
  3. Decision change: do not act on MentalArithmetic/SEED-V direction without more
     seeds; act on the four significant datasets.
- **Caveats**: summary-stats Welch CI; sample-std conversion for old values.

## figure-03-per-seed-grid — per-seed results vs baselines

- **Purpose**: expose seed-level spread and outliers behind each aggregate.
- **Plotted variables**: one panel per dataset; blue dots = new per-seed test values
  (jittered x); red line = old flatten mean with ±1 sample-σ band; gray dashed line =
  old patch-mean mean where recorded. y = primary metric.
- **Caption draft**: "Per-seed final-test values of the new recipe against recorded
  baselines; the red band is ±1 sample standard deviation of the old flatten results."
- **Key observation**: PhysioNet/FACED/ISRUC seeds sit entirely above the old band;
  SHU-MI shows one clear outlier (s45 ≈ .60) below the band; BCIC2020-3 and SEED-V
  seeds sit mostly below the old mean; MentalArithmetic spans wide both sides.
- **Interpretation checklist**:
  1. Why: audits whether means are driven by single seeds.
  2. Notice: SHU-MI's "flat" aggregate is one bad seed; ISRUC/FACED/PhysioNet are
     seed-robust.
  3. Decision change: any follow-up on SHU-MI should add seeds rather than re-tune.
- **Caveats**: old per-seed values unavailable — the band is not a true seed
  distribution; jitter is cosmetic only.
