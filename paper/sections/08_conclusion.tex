\section{Conclusion}

We introduced phase-interleaved, lossless temporal folding as a parameter-free
interface between EEG and generic vision backbones. Across 12 benchmarks, it
makes mature visual representations competitive under full fine-tuning, while
the folding and initialization controls identify when that transfer is most
credible.

Visual pretraining improves balanced accuracy in 34 of 36 matched
backbone--dataset comparisons, but EEG-specific models remain stronger on some
tasks. The practical principle is to align signal geometry and measure the
value of existing representations before assuming that larger EEG-specific
pretraining is necessary for downstream gains.
