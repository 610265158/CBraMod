\section{Detailed Results}
\label{app:detailed-results}

This appendix gives the per-dataset comparison used by the main tables. The
column order is fixed: binary tasks use BA / PR-AUC / ROC-AUC and multiclass
tasks use BA / Cohen's $\kappa$ / weighted F1. Published values are
transcribed from the \cbramod{} and \reve{} benchmark tables, including the
complete shared supervised suite and the BIOT/LaBraM EEG-foundation baselines
\citep{wang2024cbramod,elouahidi2025reve}; the local row is our finalized
EfficientNet-B0 result. ``--'' denotes an unreported dataset, not a zero.
Published and local rows can still differ in preprocessing,
optimization, seeds, and checkpoint selection, so these are direct benchmark
comparisons rather than paired reproductions.
